About My Pet

My favourite pet is my cat, an independent yet affectionate companion who has been part of my family for three years. She greets me with a soft purr and curls up beside me whenever I sit down. Caring for her has taught me responsibility and patience, and she has become one of the most important members of our household. Whether she is chasing a toy across the living room or napping peacefully in the sun, she always brings comfort and joy into my daily life.
